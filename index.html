About me

My current work is on warden, a self-hosted trading agent for BNB Smart Chain. warden is a port of an upstream trading-agent project onto BNB Smart Chain, not something I built from zero. What's mine in it: the PancakeSwap v3 venue layer, a 15-minute TWAP pricing model that refuses to trust a thinly-traded pool, and two Solidity contracts (BreakerRegistry and KernelBreakerPolicy) that trip a drawdown breaker at the account level instead of in the worker process, where a bug could get quietly skipped.

Before Solidity, four years of Python and Django — APIs, webhook signature verification, database policy hardening. Same instinct, different chain: assume the thing calling your code is lying, and check anyway.

I studied Computer Science at the University of Ibadan. Open to remote roles and contract work, and can be paid in stablecoins if that's easier on your end.
